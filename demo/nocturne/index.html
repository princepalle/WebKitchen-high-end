Our story

The Nocturne

Named for the music written for the night, The Nocturne is a modern take on dining after dark — slower, quieter, and lit by flame.

We built the room around the hours most restaurants rush through. Low light, deep chairs, and a kitchen that cooks over wood and ember, so every plate carries a little smoke and a little warmth.

The menu is short and seasonal. Ingredients come from growers we know by name. Nothing is hurried — not the bread, not the sauces, and certainly not you.

The chef

Elena Marlow,
keeper of the fire

“Fire forces you to slow down. You can't rush an ember — so we don't rush anything.”

Elena trained in the wood-fired kitchens of northern Spain and southern France before coming home to build a room of her own. Her cooking is simple on the plate and patient behind it: long braises, live-fire roasting, and sauces that start the day before.

At The Nocturne she cooks the way she likes to eat — late, by candlelight, with people worth staying up for.
