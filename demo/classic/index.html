Our story

From Cameroon to Palestine, with love

Our kitchen is run by a proud daughter of Cameroon — a pastor's wife, a mother, and a familiar, friendly face in Palestine. For years she has served our community through her African store, helping families find the foods and flavors of home.

Now she's opened a kitchen so everyone can taste the dishes she grew up on: rich ndolé, hearty eru, egusi and fufu, smoky jollof and spicy soya — alongside the Caribbean favorites our neighbors love.

Come hungry. Leave like family.
