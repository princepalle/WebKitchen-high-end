Our story

From Cameroon
to Palestine

Our founder grew up in Cameroon, where every meal was an act of welcome. A pastor's wife and a mother, she has spent years running our African store here in Palestine — helping families find the tastes of home.

The kitchen is the next chapter: the dishes she was raised on, cooked the way they should be, and shared with everyone who walks through the door.

“Come hungry. Leave like family.”
